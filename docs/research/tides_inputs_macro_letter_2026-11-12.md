## What the letter tracks

| Topic | What it watched | What it is read as |
|---|---|---|
| Inflation | Monthly core CPI (0.3%) and headline (0.4%) vs forecasts; breadth: 59% of categories rising faster than 3% a year (most since Jan 2023) | Pressure on the Fed to raise faster |
| Fed expectations | Market-implied odds of a December raise (77% → near certain; 10% for a half point) | How fast short rates will rise |
| Short rates | 2-year yield: +17 bp in a day (largest since Apr 2025) | The market's view of the Fed path |
| Demand for US debt | 10-year auction: a "tail" (sold at a higher yield than the market just before); indirect bidders (foreign central banks, overseas investors) 61.2% vs 80.3% in October, the largest drop since 2016, fifth sale in a row below their 6-month average | Fewer patient buyers, so long rates must rise to clear |
| Long rates | 10- and 30-year yields (30-year about 5.35%) | Cost of mortgages and company borrowing; pressure on equity valuations |
| The curve | Short end pushed up by the Fed, long end by buyers stepping back ("squeezed from both ends") | Long rates can rise even when the economy does not need them to |
| Gold | Fell about 2.5% on the day | Gold pays no interest, so higher real rates weigh on it |
| Event calendar | 30-year auction, producer prices, Nvidia earnings, Fed meeting (9 Dec), CPI (10 Dec), government funding deadline (11 Dec) | Known dates when the picture can change quickly |

## What it suggests for Tides

1. **Split rates into three currents, not one:** short end (2-year, Fed expectations), long end (10/30-year), and
   the curve between them. They can move for different reasons.
2. **Inflation as its own current:** monthly CPI against forecasts, and its breadth. Point-in-time data is needed
   (the value as first published, on its release date), e.g. ALFRED vintages from FRED.
3. **Demand for government debt:** auction results (indirect bidder share against its own 6-month average, and the
   tail) are public from the US Treasury, released minutes after each auction. A slow, rare signal, but a direct
   read on who is funding the long end. Feasibility to check: the tail needs the pre-auction market yield.
4. **Gold against real rates:** read gold together with real yields (inflation-protected bond yields), not alone.
5. **A macro calendar as a risk signal:** "a high-impact release within 24 hours" (Fed, CPI, jobs, big auctions,
   funding deadlines) as context for Sets and funnels, like the earnings warning we already show for equities.
6. **Fed odds:** a free route may be fed funds futures (ZQ=F on Yahoo) rather than CME FedWatch, which has no free
   feed. To check.
