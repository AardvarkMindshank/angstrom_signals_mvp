## 1. Purpose

Angstrom Signals is a proprietary financial-market analysis platform incorporating technical indicators, market intelligence, analytical research tools, configurable signal systems and related technology (the “Platform”).

The Company is inviting selected individuals to participate in its restricted, non-public testing programme (the “Private Beta”).

Participation may provide early access to confidential technology, experimental functionality, proprietary research and unreleased product features.

In consideration of being granted access, the Participant agrees to the terms below.

## 2. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

### 2.1 Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information disclosed or made available through the Private Beta, including:

- Proprietary signal calculations, algorithms, scoring methodologies, analytical models and technical architecture.
- Unreleased features, interface designs, prototypes, screenshots, demonstrations and product documentation.
- Non-public details of Lattice, Prism, Signal Groups, Signal Sets, market-scanning tools and strategy-building functionality.
- Internal research, testing results, development plans, commercial strategies and pricing information.
- Private communications and documentation provided to beta participants.

Information is confidential whether or not specifically labelled as such, where its nature or the circumstances reasonably indicate confidentiality.

### 2.2 Participant Obligations

The Participant agrees to:

- Keep Confidential Information private and use it only for authorised testing.
- Not share, publish, distribute or publicly demonstrate unreleased features or proprietary information without prior written permission.
- Not disclose screenshots, recordings, documentation or technical details to third parties without authorisation.
- Not share account credentials or allow unauthorised individuals to access the Platform.
- Take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorised access or disclosure.
- Promptly notify the Company of any known or suspected confidentiality breach.

### 2.3 Exceptions

These obligations do not apply to information that:

- Becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement.
- Was lawfully known to the Participant before disclosure.
- Is independently developed without using the Company’s Confidential Information.
- Is lawfully obtained from another source without confidentiality restrictions.
- Is approved for disclosure by the Company in writing.

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits legally protected disclosures to regulators, law enforcement authorities or other competent bodies, or disclosures required by law.

## 3. Permitted Use and Access

The Company grants the Participant a limited, personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable right to access the Private Beta solely for evaluation and testing.

Without prior written authorisation, the Participant must not:

- Reverse-engineer, decompile or attempt to extract proprietary source code or algorithms, except where such restrictions are prohibited by applicable law.
- Copy, reproduce, redistribute or commercially exploit the Platform or its Confidential Information.
- Circumvent access controls, authentication or technical restrictions.
- Conduct unauthorised security testing, automated scraping or activities that interfere with Platform operation.
- Use confidential beta materials to replicate proprietary Angstrom Signals functionality for commercial purposes.

The Participant may freely use their own trading knowledge, independently developed strategies and publicly available analytical methods.

The Company may modify, restrict, suspend or withdraw beta access at its discretion.

## 4. Intellectual Property and Feedback

All intellectual property rights in the Platform, including its software, proprietary technology, designs, branding, analytical methods and documentation, remain with the Company or its licensors.

Participation does not transfer ownership or grant rights beyond the limited testing access described in this Agreement.

## 6. Duration, Termination and Continuing Confidentiality

**6.1 Term.** This Agreement takes effect upon electronic signature and remains in force throughout the Participant’s involvement in the Private Beta.

**6.2 Termination.** Either Party may end participation. The Company may suspend or revoke access at any time, including where there is a suspected security issue, breach of this Agreement or operational requirement.

**6.3 Following Termination.** The Participant must stop using restricted beta functionality and, upon reasonable request, delete or return Confidential Information, except where retention is legally required or deletion from routine backups is not reasonably practicable.

**6.4 Survival of Confidentiality.** Confidentiality obligations continue for three (3) years after beta participation ends.

**6.5 Trade Secrets.** Information qualifying as a trade secret under applicable law remains protected for as long as it retains that legal status, subject to applicable law.

Termination does not affect accrued rights or obligations intended to continue beyond the beta programme.
