# Supabase email templates
Paste each file into Supabase > Authentication > Emails (or Email Templates), in the matching template, and set the subject shown.
- `confirm_signup.html`: subject **Confirm your Angstrom Signals email**
- `reset_password.html`: subject **Reset your Angstrom Signals password**
- `magic_link.html`: subject **Your Angstrom Signals sign-in link**
