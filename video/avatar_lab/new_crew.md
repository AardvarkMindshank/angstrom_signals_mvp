# New crew: character sheets (23 Nov 2026)

All three are original characters in the same polished 3D animated style as the rest of the crew.

## The Cook
- **Look:** rotund, big belly, rosy glowing cheeks, sweaty and flushed (just out of a steaming galley), bushy
  ginger-grey moustache, crumpled cook's hat askew.
- **Outfit and props:** a white apron smeared with grease, gravy and flour, one big chest pocket (the Rat hides in it),
  tea towel over the shoulder, wooden spoon or a half-eaten pie.
- **Personality:** jolly, flustered, always mid-recipe; loves eating his own cooking; mixes up market words with food.
- **Running gags:** turns to go at the end and the apron's back reveals bright pink heart-print boxer shorts; the Rat
  pops up from his pocket.
- **Sample lines:** "Phew! Hot in that galley. Quick forecast before the stew boils over." / "The bulls are back?
  Lovely, I'll do a roast."

## The Admiral
- **Look:** a strikingly handsome Black British admiral in his forties, strong athletic build, trimmed short beard,
  warm confident half-smile.
- **Outfit and props:** immaculate navy dress uniform, broad gold lace, epaulettes, medal ribbons and one polished gold
  medal he is very proud of; white peaked cap with gold oak-leaf braid.
- **Personality:** commanding, charming, impeccably posh English; inspects everything; the crew snap to attention.
- **Running gags:** polishes his medal mid-sentence; finds the Rat's cheese stash on inspection; outrageously
  formal turns of phrase.
- **When:** rare visits, and on big event days (Fed decisions, CPI, jobs reports).
- **Sample lines:** "Good morning, crew. The Admiral is aboard, so do stand up straight." / "The markets, I'm afraid,
  have been rather undisciplined this week."

## The Rat
- **Look:** a scruffy brown ship's stowaway, chewed left ear, tiny gold hoop earring, red-and-white spotted
  neckerchief, mischievous black eyes, crooked grin. Deliberately unlike any famous film rat: no chef, no cooking,
  no blue-grey fur, no French accent.
- **Setting:** under the chart table among his hoard: a bitten wedge of cheese, rolled old maps, brass binoculars,
  a candle stub.
- **Personality:** cheeky, quick, a whispering conspirator; forever glancing over his shoulder for the cat.
- **When:** only on days the cat isn't presenting.
- **Sample lines:** "Psst. Cat's away. I'm doing the forecast. Keep your voice down." / "Markets are nibbling
  higher. Like me and this cheese."

## Bloopers (between sections, never across a number; about one video in four)
1. A loud cat screech and a blur as the cat chases the Rat across the shot; the presenter blinks, carries on.
2. A tongue-twisted line, a recovery, and the correct line said cleanly.
3. The Rat pops out of the Cook's apron pocket, looks around, ducks back in.
4. The cat lies down across the chart table, over the numbers.
5. A big wave rolls the ship; everything slides out of shot and back.
6. The Admiral's inspection finds the cheese stash.
