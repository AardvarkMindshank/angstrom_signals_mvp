Hear from other traders

Scores for whole watchlists

Each asset gets a technical score from 0 to 100, built from RSI, Stochastic, moving averages, ADX, MACD, Ichimoku, Bollinger Bands, volume, OBV and the 7-day move. 50 is neutral, 70 and above is a strong bullish setup, 30 and below a strong bearish one.

Hover a score to read why it landed there. Slide each factor up or down, or switch it off, and save your own mix as a named set.

Market sentiment for crypto, stocks and single assets

An AI reads the last seven days of headlines for each asset and scores every one from 0 to 100, with a one-sentence summary beside it. The average becomes the asset's sentiment score. The same is done for the crypto market and the stock market, alongside public readings such as liquidity, the dollar and yields, policy and inflation, and market breadth.

Sentiment stays separate from the technical score, so you can see when the two agree and when they do not.

Add your own read, and see the crowd's

Beside every sentiment score you can agree, disagree, or give your own score from 0 to 100 with a short comment. The dashboard averages everyone's scores into a crowd reading that sits next to the AI's, so you can see where traders think the headlines are right and where they are not.

A live social stream and chat

A side panel holds a stream of short notes tied to assets and to the sentiment readings people are responding to, and a shared chat room below it. Filter by asset, by coverage (positive, negative or mixed) or by person. Star people to follow them, form groups, reply, give a note a thumb, and report anything that breaks the rules.

Signals that are tested, not just shown

For each asset, the Evidence view replays the technical score over up to five years of daily prices, without looking ahead, and shows whether higher scores really went with better prices afterwards. The verdict is in plain words, and it says so when there is no evidence.
