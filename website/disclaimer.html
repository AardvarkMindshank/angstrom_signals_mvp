What scores and tests are

Scores, sentiment readings, alerts and evidence verdicts describe past and recent data. Backtests are simulations. They use assumptions about costs and timing, they cover a limited period, and they cannot capture everything that happens in real markets. A result that held in the past may not hold again.

Risk

Crypto assets and stocks can lose value quickly, and you can lose some or all of the money you put in. Crypto markets in particular are volatile and lightly regulated. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

Your decisions

You are responsible for your own decisions. Consider speaking to a qualified, licensed adviser who knows your circumstances before you act on anything you read here.

Data and AI

Prices and other data come from third-party providers and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Sentiment scores and some wording are produced by an AI and can contain errors. Comments in the community panel are written by users and are not reviewed or endorsed by us.
