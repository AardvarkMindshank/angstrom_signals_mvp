Evidence: how ideas are tested

A rule is only worth watching if what followed it in the past was different from an ordinary day. Angstrom Signals lets you check, and tells you plainly when it can't.

Readings describe, they don't predict

Trend, Stretch, Flow and sentiment describe conditions now. None of them has been shown to predict which way price goes next, and the app says so beside them. Testing is how you find out whether a combination of them has meant anything in the past.

🔬 What followed: studying a Set

Press Study this Set on any Set to see what happened after it turned Yes in the past.

No looking ahead
Every past answer is worked out again using only the prices known at that moment, the same way the Set would have answered live.
Ten years, many assets
September 2014 to October 2024, on every studied coin and equity, at several horizons afterwards.
Against ordinary days
Moves after a Yes are compared with comparable ordinary days, because most assets drift one way and a raw average flatters any rule.
Three periods side by side
A result has to hold across separate stretches of history, not only on average.
Coins and equities apart
Each market is shown on its own, so one cannot hide the other.
A luck check
With many assets, some always look unusual by chance. Asset by asset, the study marks which results stand out beyond what luck alone produces, and treats those as a place to look closer, not as proof.

What the verdict means

The verdict comes from fixed rules applied to the numbers, not from opinion or AI. It is one of:

  • Too few to judge: the Set said Yes too rarely to tell anything.
  • No clear difference: what followed a Yes looked like an ordinary day.
  • Some difference, not established: there is a difference, but it does not hold up across periods or clear the luck check.
  • Consistent difference: the difference held across periods and cleared the luck check.

Each provided Group and Set carries its study mark, or 🔬 when it has not been studied yet and only describes conditions.

🔒 A protected hold-out

Studies stop at the end of October 2024. Everything after that is held back and never used to measure or tune anything, so there is always fresh history for a final, honest check of an idea that looks promising. You can still view recent history on the chart; it just isn't used for testing.

🕰️ Seeing it on the chart

Visualize History draws a Set's past answers as coloured bands behind the price chart, green for Yes and amber for Unknown, across the whole history, so you can see with your own eyes where a rule fired and what price did next.

New data is tested before it is used

New kinds of information, such as funding rates or options positioning, are checked on history before they appear as signals. If a test is unclear, the app says it was not clear, rather than hinting at an effect.

Limits

  • History is not the future. A difference that held can stop holding.
  • Studies show past patterns before trading costs.
  • Short histories and rare signals give weak tests, and the study says when that is the case.
