Read the market

Trend, Stretch and Flow for every asset on your watchlists, news sentiment read by AI, and a Quick Snapshot of what changed since you last looked.

🧩

Build your own rules

In the Lattice workspace, combine signals into Groups (your own reading) and Sets (a Yes or No decision), including "this, then that within N candles".

🔎

Scan and filter

Prism scans your watchlist with any Set, now or on a schedule, with alerts. Funnels narrow a whole market from the daily chart down to the hour.

🔬

Check what followed

Study any Set against ten years of history, with a luck check, and see its past Yes days as bands on the chart. Recent data is held back so tests stay honest.

🌊

Know the tide

Tides tracks twelve macro currents (rates, the dollar, gold, credit, oil, crypto rotation and more), the sea state, and the events on the calendar.

💬

Trade ideas with people

A live stream and chat, communities, private messages, and community votes beside every AI sentiment score.

✨ AI Navigator

Built in, on every page.

Ask where something is, what a reading means, or how to turn an idea into a Group, Set or scan, and the Navigator walks you through it. It knows the whole app, your watchlist, your Groups, Sets and funnels, today's Tides and the asset you have open. Paste a screenshot and ask what you are looking at.

Each morning, Today's AI Passage Plan sets out the conditions, the week's events and what to watch on your own watchlist and funnels. It describes. It never tells you to buy or sell.

Honest by design

Readings describe conditions now. None of them has been shown to predict which way price goes next, and the app says so. When you study a Set, the verdict comes from fixed rules and a luck check, not from opinion, and "too few to tell" is a common answer. How the testing works.
