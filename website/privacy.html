Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This explains what Angstrom Signals stores about you and who helps us run it. Angstrom Signals is operated by Angus Marshall, a sole trader based in Thailand, who is responsible for your data. Contact: info@angstromsignals.com.

What we store

  • Your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture.
  • What you save in the dashboard: watchlists, factor weights, alert rules, chart choices and settings.
  • What you post: community notes, chat messages (public, community and private), thumbs, scores you give, and any communities, favourites and blocks you set up.
  • What you ask the AI Navigator, and its answers, so you can return to the conversation.
  • If you buy a paid plan, your plan, its status and renewal date. Card and payment details are taken by Paddle and never reach us.
  • Your sign-in session, which your browser keeps so you stay signed in.
  • If you use the contact form, the name, email address, phone, country and message you type. They are emailed to us and kept in our inbox.
  • If you ask for beta access, what you type in the request form: your name, email address, telephone number, country, the markets you trade, how many years you have traded, how active you are, the tools you use, and your reasons for wanting access. See "Beta access requests" below.

Beta access requests

Angstrom Signals is invite-only while we build with experienced traders. We use what you put in the request form only to decide whether to offer you a seat, to contact you about it, and to understand who our beta traders are. The request is emailed to the small team that runs Angstrom Signals and saved in our database, where only administrators can read it. We do not use your telephone number for marketing, and we do not share your request with anyone outside the services listed below.

We keep a request while it is being considered and for as long as you hold a seat or an account. You can ask us to see or delete your request at any time by emailing info@angstromsignals.com. Declining a request does not stop you applying again later.

What other people can see

Your name and picture appear beside community notes and chat messages you post. A public message can be read by every member; a community message by that community's members; a private message by you and the person you sent it to. Your watchlists are private. Other people only see how many accounts watch an asset, not who.

Messages and moderation

Messages are stored on our database, encrypted in transit and at rest, but not end-to-end encrypted. When a message is reported, administrators can read it, and the conversation around it, to decide whether it breaks the rules. Administrators can also look at messages when the law requires it or to deal with abuse, but we do not read private messages otherwise.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts and data, including beta requests, and handles sign-in.
  • HostGator hosts this website and sends the emails from its contact and beta request forms.
  • Render hosts the dashboard.
  • Paddle.com is our reseller and Merchant of Record: it takes payments, handles tax, invoices and refunds, and keeps your billing details under its own privacy notice.
  • Anthropic (Claude) answers questions you ask the AI Navigator. What you type there, and the readings it needs, are sent to answer you; they are not used to train its models.
  • GitHub runs our background jobs (alerts, scans and funnels) on stored market data.
  • Resend sends emails, such as alerts and sign-in messages.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • xAI processes public news headlines to produce sentiment scores. Your personal details are not sent for this.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your data, or to close your account, email info@angstromsignals.com.
