Good to know

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account or the link in any receipt. Your plan runs to the end of the period you have paid for, then the account moves to Free.

What happens to my work if I move to a lower plan?

Nothing is deleted. Groups, Sets, scans and funnels above the new plan's limits are paused and kept, and come back when you upgrade again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you are not happy, ask within 14 days of a payment for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

Who takes the payment?

Paddle.com is our online reseller and the Merchant of Record for every order. Paddle handles checkout, invoices, tax and refunds, and the charge on your statement will name Paddle.

Is this financial advice?

No. MarketNavi is a research tool. Read the not-financial-advice notice.
