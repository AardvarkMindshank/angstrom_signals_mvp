Questions and answers

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Is it free?

It is free for accepted beta traders. Seats are limited and we expect feedback in return. When paid plans begin there will still be a Free plan, and we will give at least 30 days' notice first. See pricing.

Can I get a refund on a paid plan?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of any payment for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

How do I join the beta?

Use the Request beta access form. We read every request and invite people in small groups, so not everyone will get a seat straight away. Accepted traders sign a short beta agreement before they start.

Does it tell me what to buy or sell?

No. It describes conditions and lets you test whether your own rules have meant anything in the past. Decisions are yours. Read the not-financial-advice notice.

What are Trend, Stretch and Flow?

Three technical readings for every asset, each from −100 to +100. Trend is which way and how firmly price is moving, Stretch is how far it has run, and Flow is whether volume backs the move. They are kept separate and never added together.

What are Groups, Sets and Prism?

They are the parts of the Lattice workspace. A Group combines signals into your own reading. A Set turns readings into a Yes or No decision. Prism scans your watchlist with a Set, now or on a schedule. A funnel chains Sets from the daily chart down to the hour.

Do I have to build everything myself?

No. There is a library of provided Groups, Sets, scans and funnels built around common setups. You can use them as they are, look inside to see how they work, or copy one and change it. The AI Navigator can walk you through building your own.

What is the community view?

Beside each AI sentiment score you can agree, disagree or give your own score with a short comment. Everyone's votes are averaged into a community reading shown next to the AI's.

What is in the social panel?

A stream of short notes tied to assets and sentiment, and a chat. Messages can go to everyone, to a community you belong to, or privately to one person. You choose who can message you and can block anyone.

Is the chat moderated?

Any message can be reported, and accounts that break the rules can be barred. Administrators can read a reported message to deal with it. See the privacy notice.

Which markets does it cover?

Crypto and equities. You choose what goes on your watchlists, and funnels can search the top coins and a broad list of equities.

Where does the data come from?

Crypto prices come from Binance, equity prices from Yahoo Finance, and options data from Deribit and Yahoo. Macro readings for Tides come from public sources. Prices are saved about every five minutes and shared by all accounts.

What does the AI do?

Two things. An AI reads recent headlines and scores each one for sentiment. The AI Navigator answers questions about the app and your research, and writes the daily Passage Plan. Study verdicts never come from AI: they come from fixed rules applied to the numbers.

Why does a study so often say there is no clear difference?

Because that is what tests of trading rules often find. Prices are noisy, and most simple rules do not beat an ordinary day. Saying so is the point.

How do alerts work?

Scans, funnels and the built-in reading alerts send alerts in the app and by email, as they happen or in a daily summary. They keep running when you are logged out.

Can other people see my watchlists or rules?

No. Your watchlists, Groups, Sets, scans and funnels are private unless you choose to share them. Others can see how many accounts watch an asset, not who.

Which languages does it support?

Twelve, including English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish and Thai. It follows your browser's language, and a flag switches it.

How do I sign in?

Once you have been accepted, with your email and a password, or with Google.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the Feedback box in the app. We are a start-up and keen for new ideas, and we will do our best to respond.

How do I delete my account?

Email info@angstromsignals.com.
