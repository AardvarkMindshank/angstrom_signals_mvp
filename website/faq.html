Questions and answers

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Is it free?

It is free for accepted beta traders. Seats are limited and we expect feedback in return. When paid plans begin there will still be a Free plan, and we will give at least 30 days' notice first. See pricing.

Can I get a refund on a paid plan?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of any payment for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

How do I join the beta?

Use the Request beta access form. We read every request and invite people in small groups, so not everyone will get a seat straight away.

Does it tell me what to buy or sell?

No. It describes recent conditions and tests whether its own scores have helped on past prices. Decisions are yours. Read the not-financial-advice notice.

What is the crowd view?

Beside each sentiment score you can agree, disagree or give your own score from 0 to 100 with a short comment. Everyone's scores are averaged into a crowd reading shown next to the AI's.

What is the social stream?

A side panel with short notes tied to assets and to sentiment readings, plus a shared chat. You can filter by asset, coverage or person, follow people, form groups, reply and report anything that breaks the rules.

Is the stream moderated?

Any comment can be reported, and accounts that break the rules can be barred. Report problems with the flag beside a comment.

Which markets does it cover?

Crypto coins and stocks. You choose what goes on your watchlists.

Where do the prices come from?

Prices come mainly from Yahoo Finance, with CoinGecko used to look up coins, show market size and as a backup for crypto. They are saved about every five minutes and shared by all accounts.

What does the AI do?

It reads recent headlines for an asset or a whole market and scores each one from 0 to 100 with a one-sentence summary, and it helps word the Evidence paragraph. The Evidence verdict itself comes from fixed rules applied to the test numbers, and the AI's wording is checked against them.

Why does it so often say there is no evidence?

Because that is what tests of simple technical scores often find. Prices are noisy, and most indicators do not reliably beat holding. Saying so is the point.

How do alerts work?

You get one email when a technical score crosses a level you set. It is not sent again until the score has left that level and crossed it later.

Can I see other people's watchlists?

No. You can see how many accounts watch an asset, but not who they are.

How do I sign in?

Once you have been accepted, with your email and a password, or with Google. The sign-in address is on your invitation.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the Feedback box in the dashboard's sidebar. We are a start-up and keen for new ideas, and we will do our best to respond.

How do I delete my account?

Email info@angstromsignals.com.
