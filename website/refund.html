Refund policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with a paid MarketNavi plan for any reason, ask within 14 days of the payment and you will get a full refund of that payment. No questions asked.

This applies to every payment: a first subscription, a renewal, monthly or yearly, and an upgrade to a higher plan.

How to ask for a refund

  • Reply to your receipt email from Paddle, or use the link in it, or go to paddle.net to find your order; or
  • Email us at info@marketnavi.ai with the email address you paid with.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Paddle usually processes them within a few days; your bank may take a little longer to show it.

After a refund

Your account moves to the Free plan. Nothing you made is deleted: anything above the Free limits is paused and kept, and comes back if you subscribe again.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again. Your plan runs to the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling on its own does not refund that period, but if the payment was within the last 14 days you can ask for a refund as above.

Who handles payments

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns. Your rights under Paddle's buyer terms apply as well as this policy.

Contact
